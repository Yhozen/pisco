doomgeneric + Freedoom, built against D3D12.X, GameInput, XAudio2 and XGameSave; its x86-64 code recompiled to
WebAssembly, D3D12.X translated to WebGPU, XAudio2 mixed into an AudioWorklet, saves kept in the browser (OPFS).
Recorded in headless Chrome with WebGPU on SwiftShader (no GPU); every frame was read back from WebGPU. Shows: title
and attract demo, a new game from scripted keys, the automap, a save, a page reload (a new title process), the save
loaded back.
