The first Windows PC title, a 32-bit Direct3D 9 game, played by hand in Chrome on an M1 MacBook Pro
([video](https://github.com/Yhozen/pisco/releases/tag/narbacular-drop-video-2026-11-25)). What it took: the
recompiler's 32-bit mode with x87, a Win32 process for a 32-bit guest, fixed-function Direct3D 9 on WebGPU, GDI text,
FMOD 3 on an AudioWorklet, DirectInput from the keyboard and a Pointer Lock mouse, saves in OPFS, and recipes that
build title images. 103–355 frames a second on that machine.
