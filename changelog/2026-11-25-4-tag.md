DigiPen's paint-gun game (2008), with Microsoft's own D3DX from its installer lifted beside it: its 16 effects compile
at run time to the same 46 shaders as under Wine, Direct3D 9 shader bytecode is translated to WGSL, FMOD Ex's calls
are Wine's, and in headless Chrome its first level is within 1.4 of 255 of Wine's screenshots on average.
