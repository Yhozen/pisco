title: A fourth runtime: Linux x64, with fastfetch
prs: PR #31
---
Linux at its system calls, for one statically linked program: files, memory, the clock, a generated `/proc`.
The recompiler reads ELF; the core's system-neutral parts moved to files of their own. fastfetch 2.71.0 prints the
machine it finds, a Linux x86-64 system imitated in WebAssembly, with its logo and colours, in Node and in the page.
