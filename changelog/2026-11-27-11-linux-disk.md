---
title: A disk for the lifted Linux
---
The `microvm-x64` machine has a disk: virtio's block device on its MMIO transport. With `SW_DISK=16M` (zeros) or `SW_DISK=<file>`, the lifted Linux finds `/dev/vda`. `/init` formats it as ext2 with busybox, writes 1,000 lines, mounts it again and reads them back. It worked on its first run.

- **`rt/microvm-x64/virtio.c`:** virtio 1.2's MMIO registers, one queue, reads, writes, flush and the disk's id, interrupts through the IOAPIC's pin 5. The runtime announces the device on the kernel's command line.
- **The kernel** gains virtio's MMIO transport, its block driver and ext2. Without a disk, `/init` still prints what it prints on QEMU.
- **`tests/run.sh kernel`** checks the disk too.

Still missing: the disk's writes are kept in memory, not in its file; there is no network.
