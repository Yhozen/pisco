title: cowsay, on perl
prs: PR #37
---
cowsay 3.8.4 on perl 5.44, built from source as a static Linux program: six runs (cowsay and cowthink, several
cows) print what native perl prints, in Node and in Chrome. The port found two recompiler gaps that a newer compiler
(gcc 13) exposes: a `switch` table whose bound is far from its jump, and `notrack` jumps through
tables. Without the fix, perl's parser was lifted as a recursion that overflowed Chrome's worker stack. Also
included, as an experimental option: `tools/boat.sh` runs the test steps on several boat.dev sandboxes at
once.
