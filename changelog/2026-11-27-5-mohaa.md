---
title: Medal of Honor: Allied Assault's demo, an OpenGL game
---
The single-player demo of Medal of Honor: Allied Assault (2001), on Ritual's version of the Quake III engine, on the
Windows x86 runtime: the logos, the menu, the difficulty, the briefing and its level, with Miles' sound. In headless
Chrome the menus are within 0.4 of 255 of Wine's screenshots on average and the level's first view within 2.6; its
sound is within 0.3 dB of Wine's recording.

- **OpenGL 1.1 on WebGPU**: immediate mode and client arrays, two texture units with their environments as WGSL,
  alpha test, fog, `glDrawPixels`, `glReadPixels`.
- **`pisco build`** reads a Wise installer and the InstallShield 6 setup inside it, and lifts the DLLs a title loads
  itself (the game, the client game, Miles and its plugins).
- **The lifter** lifts MMX; **the runtime** gets DirectSound, input as window messages, DLLs loaded on demand.
